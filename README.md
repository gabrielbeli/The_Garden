# The Garden - A Console Text RPG

💻 Final Project for Object-Oriented Programming with Java 🎮

**The Garden** is an immersive console-based text RPG where narrative depth meets strategic gameplay. Developed as a final project for an Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) course, this game heavily leverages advanced Java concepts, such as **Inheritance, Encapsulation, Polymorphism, and Abstraction**, to manage complex storylines, character archetypes, branching choices, and turn-based combat mechanics.

## 📜 Story & Lore

Legends say that in the ancient past, two noble warrior brothers fought a tragic battle for the throne of the Garden Kingdom. **Dandelion**, the legendary "Golden Prince," managed to subdue his brother, **Thorn**, known as "Dark Justice." However, despite his victory, Dandelion was struck by Thorn's lethal poison, succumbing to death years later. Left without a crown, the Garden entered an era of profound sorrow known as **The Great Mourning**.

While the loyal subjects tried their best to protect the land, they could not fully withstand the dark malice of the witch **Belladonna**, the former princess betrothed to Prince Thorn. Driven mad by grief and vengeful fury after Thorn's demise, Belladonna swore to completely destroy the Garden. Recognizing her dark potential, her mentor **Bromelia**, the protective arch-witch, bound her powers and banished her to the southern **Poisonous Swamp**.

Centuries have passed, and the Garden is on the brink of total extinction. Bromelia's magical life force is rapidly fading, meaning Belladonna will soon break free to fulfill her ultimate revenge. The only hope lies in finding a new worthy ruler. Bromelia and the remaining citizens have summoned those possessing true courage, loyalty, and goodness. Many have tried, but the Throne demands a soul chosen directly by the Spirit of the Garden.

As the chosen seed, you must journey through fractured lands:
* **The Poppy & Rose Fields:** Once beautiful regions that succumbed to pride and ego, now serving as perilous strongholds for the enemy.
* **The Lavender & Daisy Clearings:** Peaceful havens unaffected by the ancient war, offering friendship, respite, and bountiful harvests to well-intentioned travelers.
* **The Thorny Threshold (Umbral Espinhento):** The legendary battleground where the tragic fate of the brothers was sealed.

If you fail, there will be no second chance, and the Garden will fade into eternal darkness.

## 🚀 Game Features

* **Rich Text Narrative:** A deep focus on storytelling, descriptive lore, and impactful dialogue where every choice shapes the world.
* **Character Customization:** Name your hero and strategically distribute initial attribute points across **Health**, **Strength**, and **Currency**.
* **Dynamic Class Progression:** Choose your specialized character archetype/category at a critical junction in your journey.
* **Turn-Based Combat System:** Battle increasingly powerful enemies using a refined turn-based system featuring standard attacks, powerful special abilities, and tactical maneuvers.
* **Interactive NPCs:** Meet various characters along the way who can fight alongside you as allies, trade valuable resources, help you level up your attributes, or offer unique quest items.
* **Inventory & Item Management:** Collect, manage, and utilize an array of items, snacks, and equipment to survive the harsh environments.
* **Branching Paths & Secret Endings:** Your decisions dictate the outcome. Discover multiple alternate endings triggered by specific ethical choices or hidden mechanical combinations.

## 🛠 Technologies Used

* **Java:** Core development language.
* **Object-Oriented Programming (OOP):** Thorough utilization of classes, inheritance, polymorphism, abstraction, and strict encapsulation to cleanly structure characters, distinct items, environmental regions, and modular combat states.
* **Console Interface:** Pure text-driven environment designed to deliver an authentic, retro desktop RPG experience.

## 🎮 How to Play

### Prerequisites
Ensure you have the Java Development Kit (JDK) installed on your system.

### Execution Steps
1. **Clone the Repository:**
```bash
   git clone https://github.com/gabrielbeli/The_Garden.git
```
2. **Navigate to the project directory:**
```bash
   cd The_Garden
```
3. **Compile the project:**
```bash
   javac src/Main.java
```
4. **Run the game:**
```bash
   java src.Main
```
Follow the on-screen narrative prompts and make your choices wisely to determine the ultimate fate of the Garden.
